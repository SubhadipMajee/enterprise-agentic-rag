# Terms of use

This software is provided as an internal or customer documentation assistant. It does not replace professional, legal, or operational advice.

## Acceptable use

You may use the assistant to ask questions about documentation your organization has authorized for retrieval.

You may not:

- Attempt to bypass safety rules, extract system prompts, or probe other users' conversations
- Upload or ask the system to process unlawful content
- Share your access password or API credentials
- Rely on answers as the sole source of truth for production changes without checking the source documents

## Accuracy

Answers are generated by a language model using retrieved chunks. They can be incomplete or wrong. Verify important steps in the original documents.

## Availability and limits

The service may enforce rate limits, maximum question length, and topic restrictions. Access may be suspended if those limits are abused.

## Operator responsibilities

The deploying organization is responsible for which documents are indexed, user access, vendor configuration, and compliance with applicable law.
